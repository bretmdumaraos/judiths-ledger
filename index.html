Judith's Ledger

A private Android app to keep track of what your family owes you, what you can still spend, and which bills are due, all stored on your phone.

Your data stays on your phone. Nothing you enter is uploaded to us or shared with advertisers.

What it does

Family

  • Create an account for each family member and list their purchases on your credit cards.
  • Installments (for example 36 months for ₱50,000 with interest), loans, fixed monthly amounts (for example a lot for 60 months) and one-time split bills (for example a Jollibee meal).
  • Recurring items are added automatically every month until the last term, such as 36 of 36.
  • Monthly electric and other changing bills are entered each month.
  • Credit-card-style PDF statements with previous balance, payments, amount due and installment progress.

My Budget

  • Salary, extra income, bills, card statements, daily and online purchases.
  • Planned purchases such as vacation, travel, shoes or bags.
  • Daily food and transport allowance and how much is left.
  • Shows how much you can still spend, and how much extra income you would need over 1, 3 or 6 months if you overspend.

Bills

  • Meralco, Netflix, HBO, credit card dues and any other bill, with search.
  • Phone reminders 3 days before, 2 days before and on the due date.
  • Mark bills as paid and see what is overdue or due soon.
