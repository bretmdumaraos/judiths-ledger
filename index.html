Good to know

Questions, answered.

The essentials about your records, reminders, payments and backups.

Does Judith’s Ledger store my records online?

No. The family, budgeting and bill information you enter is stored on your device. If you create a backup or PDF and choose to save it somewhere else, that exported file goes to the location you selected. Google AdMob separately processes ad-related device information.

Can I generate statements for family members?

Yes. Open a family account and use the PDF icon to create a monthly statement, including charges, payments and balances. You can share the exported file using an app of your choice.

Does the app remind me before bills are due?

Yes. When notifications are allowed, it can remind you before payment deadlines. The schedule depends on your settings and your phone’s notification and battery restrictions.

Can I transfer my ledger to a new phone?

Yes. Create a backup file in the app’s Settings, store it safely, then restore it on your new phone. Restoring replaces existing records in the app, so check your backup first.

Where can I get the Android app?

The supplied app information describes Google Play availability. An official store URL has not yet been added to this website. You can explore the screenshots and user guide in the meantime.
