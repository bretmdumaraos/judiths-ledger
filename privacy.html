Privacy policy

Your privacy matters.

How Judith’s Ledger handles the information you enter, exports, advertising and device permissions.

Last updated: October 13, 2026 · Judith’s Ledger

Judith's Ledger ("the app") is a personal ledger for tracking family billing, your budget and your bills. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Your ledger data stays on your phone

Everything you enter (people, charges, payments, salary, purchases, bills, reminders and settings) is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Backup files and PDF statements are created only when you choose to, and saved or shared only where you choose.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. Your ledger data is never shared with Google or advertisers.

Where required by law (for example in the EU or UK), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Learn how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app reminds you of bill due dates. Reminders are created on your device.

Files and permissions

The app saves PDF statements to your Downloads folder and backup files to a location you pick. It does not read your contacts, photos, location or other apps.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored by it on your device. You can also delete individual entries inside the app. Files you exported (backups and PDFs) are separate and stay until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
