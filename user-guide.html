User guide

Get the most from your ledger.

Step-by-step help for family accounts, PDF statements, My Budget, bills and reminders.

The app has three tabs at the bottom: Family, My Budget and Bills. Settings is the gear icon on the Family tab.

1. Family accounts

  1. On the Family tab, tap Add person and add a person (for example your sister) and her payment due day.
  2. Open her account and tap Add charge. Choose what kind of charge it is:
    • Installment purchase: enter the price, number of months and interest. The app works out the monthly bill.
    • Loan: enter the amount, months and interest.
    • Fixed monthly: you set the monthly amount and number of months.
    • One-time or split bill: enter the amount, or the whole bill and the number of people. It goes on next month's statement by default.
    • Utility bill (such as electric): enter the amount each month, since it changes.
  3. Recurring charges appear on every monthly statement automatically until the last term, for example 36 of 36.
  4. When she pays, record it in the Payments tab of her account. Anything unpaid carries over as previous balance.

2. PDF statements

  1. Open an account and choose the month.
  2. Tap the PDF icon at the top of the account screen. The statement is saved in Downloads/JudithsLedger and can be shared by Messenger, Viber or email.
  3. In Settings, set your name and a How to pay note (such as your GCash number) so they print on the statement.

3. My Budget

  • On the My Budget tab, tap Add. Add your salary (every month) and any extra income (one month only).
  • Add recurring bills, utilities, credit card statements, and your daily and online purchases.
  • Add planned purchases (for example vacation, travel, shoes or bags). Tick them off when bought.
  • Set a daily allowance for food and transport to see what is left today and this month.
  • The top card shows how much you can still spend. If you overspend, the Extra income you need card shows how much to earn extra over the next 1, 3 or 6 months.

4. Bills and reminders

  1. On the Bills tab, tap Add bill and pick a quick option (Meralco, Netflix, HBO Max and more) or type your own. Enter the due day of the month.
  2. Choose Same amount every month (Netflix) or Changes every month (Meralco, credit cards). For changing bills, tap the bill each month and enter the amount.
  3. Use the search box at the top to find a bill.
  4. Tap the circle to mark a bill paid.
  5. Allow notifications when asked. You'll be reminded around 9:00 AM, 3 days before, 2 days before and on the due date, until you mark it paid.

If reminders don't arrive, check that notifications are allowed for the app and that battery saver isn't blocking it. Settings has a Send a test reminder button.

5. Back up your data

Your data is stored only on this phone. In Settings, tap Back up to a file now and then, and keep the file in Google Drive or send it to yourself. To move to a new phone, install the app and tap Restore from a backup file. Restoring replaces everything currently in the app.

6. Ads

The app shows one small banner ad above the bottom tabs to stay free. Where required, you'll be asked for consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
